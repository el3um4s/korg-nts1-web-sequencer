Come impostare i suoni sul Korg NTS-1 fisico a casa: Guida Logue-SDK / MIDI CC

1. [OSC] & [FILTER]:

• OSC Type: Tieni premuto [OSC] e gira la manopola [TYPE].

• Shape & Alt: Gira Knob A (Shape) e Knob B (Alt).

• FILTER: Premi [FILTER]. Knob A = Cutoff (CC 43), Knob B = Resonance (CC 44).

2. [EG] & [MOD]:

• EG Type: Tieni premuto [EG] e gira [TYPE] per ADSR / AHR / AR.

• Attack & Release: Knob A = Attack (CC 16), Knob B = Release (CC 19).

• MOD: Tieni [MOD] per Chorus/Ensemble/Phaser; Knob A = Speed (CC 28), Knob B = Depth (CC 29).

3. [DELAY], [REVERB] & [ARP]:

• DELAY: Tieni [DELAY] per Stereo/Mono; Knob A = Time (CC 30), Knob B = Depth (CC 31).

• REVERB: Tieni [REVERB] per Hall/Plate/Space; Knob A = Time (CC 34), Knob B = Depth (CC 35).

• ARP: Premi [ARP], usa [TYPE] per Up/Down/Bounce; Knob A = Pattern, Knob B = Length.

• Riga Alta: Q W E R T Y U (C..B), 2 3 5 6 7 (Diesis #)
